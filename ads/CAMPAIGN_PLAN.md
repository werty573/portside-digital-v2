## 6. KPIs & decision rules

| KPI | Target | If it misses after 7 days |
|---|---|---|
| **CTR (link)** | **> 1%** | Below 0.7%: the creative isn't stopping the scroll. Pause it and test the next one from the bench. |
| **CPC (link)** | Under US$0.40 (T&T CPCs usually run US$0.10–0.40) | High CPC with OK CTR means the audience is too narrow. Remove interests. |
| **Cost per lead / conversation** | Under US$5 to start | Good CTR but no leads means the problem is after the click. Check reply speed, the greeting message and the form. |
| Reply time | < 15 minutes in business hours | Turn on WhatsApp Business away/greeting messages |
| Lead → paid project | 1 in 10–15 | Tighten the qualifying questions (add the budget question) |
| Frequency | < 3.0 per week | Above 3: rotate in fresh creatives from the bench |

**Weekly routine:** pause the bottom 2 ads by cost per result, then add 2 new ones from `AD_COPY.md`. Prefer a new angle, so if "Free offer" is winning, try its B/C sibling on a different creative.
